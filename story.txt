Once upon a time there was a dog and a cat running away from animal control.
The dog had a limp and the cat helped the the dog get to safety.
Louisa and Gemma both liked to jump over obstacles and get pets from strangers.
As the sun went down, Louisa and Gemma ran into a quiet alley. 
Animal control was gone, but they were still excited.
They stopped at a park where kids played with swings.
A friendly person on a bench saw them and waved them over. 
The person gave them a bite of a sandwich and patted them.
Feeling safe and happy, the dog and cat rested together, ready for a new day.
